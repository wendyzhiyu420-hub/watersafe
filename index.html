OUR MISSION

Making water-safety knowledge accessible to everyone.

WaterSafe is dedicated to making reliable, accessible water-safety education available to communities everywhere. Through community education, digital resources, and partnerships, we aim to equip people with the knowledge and confidence to recognize water-related risks, make safer decisions, and connect with opportunities to develop essential water-safety skills.

EMERGENCY BASICS

Reach. Throw. Don't go.

If someone is in trouble in the water, protect yourself first. Do not enter the water unless you are trained to perform a rescue.

Call 911 for emergency help.
01

The Golden Rule

Never put yourself in danger to rescue someone else. Stay on land and use a safe rescue method.

02

Reach with a pole

From a safe position on shore, extend a pole or another suitable reaching aid toward the person so they can grab it.

03

Throw a buoyancy aid

Throw a buoyancy aid, such as a life ring, toward the person. Do not throw yourself into the water.

04

Never enter unless trained

Water rescues can be dangerous. Never enter the water to perform a rescue unless you are appropriately trained.
